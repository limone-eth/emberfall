# Hero sprites: keep 16 px per tile, or move to 32 px?

Decided: 2026-10-31 · Case: c_a59553f9-6e69-4853-b0e2-5f6507584794

## Question and choice
GPT Image 2 drew the hero drafts on a 28 to 35 px grid, not 16 px. Do we squeeze them to 16 px per tile, or move to 32 px?

Choice: "Move to 32 px per tile for sprites and tiles, and approve these two sprites as the style target".

## Why
- The image model draws naturally at about 30 px. 32 px keeps the detail (face, glowing eyes, claws) with almost no hand work.
- At 16 px the face and the rod glow turned to mush, and each sprite would need a hand redraw.
- The result is still crisp pixel art, drawn at 1x scale on screen.

## Constraints
- This record replaces the "16×16 px per tile" line in `2026-10-31-art-direction.md`. All other lines there still apply.
- Tiles and sprites use 32 px per tile. Normal sprites fit in 32×32. Large enemies use 64×64; the boss uses 96×96.
- The style target is `packages/client/public/sprites/emberwright.png` and `tidecrab.png` (cleaned with `tools/sprite-clean.mjs --size 32`). New sprites must match their outline, palette and detail level.
- Hitboxes do not change.

## Ruled out
- Keep 16 px per tile: hand work on every sprite, and the drafts lose most of their charm.
- New drafts with changed prompts: not needed, the drafts are good.

## Open questions
